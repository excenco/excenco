ExcenCo. Institute of Professionals Official Affiliation & FAQ

Are we a part of Imperial College London?

Yes. Established in 2026, our institution was previously known as Dynamic Features University of London (UOL). Effective September 18, 2026, we proudly operate under Imperial College London as our parent and mother company, with the University of Adelaide serving as our official institutional sponsor.

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll in our programs by visiting our official Course Enrollment Form section on this page.

What are the accepted payment methods?

We accept secure payments via Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Binance, Bank Transfers, and bKash.

How do I sign in with my Student ID?

You can securely sign in using your assigned official Student ID and password in our Student Login Index portal section.

How can I verify my certificate?

Verify your official course completion certificate authenticity under the Verify Certificate section using secure code standard TF.990 x509.
